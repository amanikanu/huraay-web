**Huraay**

**Product Overview**

Huraay is a celebration and memory platform that brings wishes, memories, gifts, and wishlists into one place.

During birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, graduations, baby showers, promotions, retirements, and other special moments, people usually send heartfelt messages through WhatsApp, email, SMS, social media, and phone calls.

The problem is that these messages are scattered across different platforms. Some get buried in chats, some are deleted, and others become difficult to find after the celebration.

Huraay gives the celebrant one central place where all these memories can live permanently.

A celebrant can create a Wish Board for an occasion and share the board link with friends, family, colleagues, and community members.

People can then create personalized Wish Cards containing messages, photos, videos, gifts, or contributions.

The result is a digital memory board that the celebrant can revisit at any time.

**The Problem**

Celebrations generate meaningful messages and memories, but there is currently no simple central place to collect them.

**Common problems include**:

\- Birthday wishes are scattered across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, SMS, email, and phone calls.  
\- Important messages get buried or lost.  
\- Celebrants cannot easily preserve all their messages as memories.  
\- People may want to send wishes privately.  
\- Some people may want to remain anonymous.  
\- Friends often do not know what gift the celebrant wants.  
\- Celebrants may receive unwanted or duplicate gifts.  
\- Group contributions for gifts can be difficult to coordinate.  
\- There is no structured way to combine messages, memories, wishlists, and gifts around one celebration.

**The Solution**

Huraay creates a central celebration page around a person or event.

The celebrant creates a Wish Board and shares a unique link.

Anyone with access to the link can send a Wish Card.

Depending on the settings selected by the wisher, the message can be:

\- Public.  
\- Private to the celebrant.  
\- Anonymous.  
\- Scheduled for a particular date.

The celebrant can also create a wishlist containing items they would like to receive.

**Friends can:**

\- Buy an item.  
\- Reserve an item.  
\- Contribute toward an item.  
\- Send money toward a gift.  
\- Mark an item as purchased.

This reduces duplicate gifts and makes gifting easier.

CRITICAL WISHLIST GATING RULE

Visitors must submit a birthday wish before they can access protected wishlist information.

This rule must be enforced at the backend level.

Do not implement this only using frontend state.

A visitor should not be able to inspect the frontend, call Supabase directly or change a local variable to retrieve protected wishlist information.

After successful wish submission:

1. Validate the wish server-side.  
2. Create the wish.  
3. Generate a secure page-specific visitor access token.  
4. Store only a hash of that token.  
5. Return the token to the visitor.  
6. Store it safely on the client.  
7. Require it when requesting protected wishlist information.  
8. Validate it server-side.  
9. Return only wishlist information belonging to that Birthday Page.

Access should be page-specific.

Submitting a wish on one page must not unlock another page.

# **1\. Executive Summary**

The product is a mobile-first birthday wishlist and social wish-wall platform designed around how people already celebrate birthdays online.

A Page Owner creates a personal Birthday Page containing:

* Their birthday date  
* Photos  
* Wishlist items  
* Purchase links  
* Optional cash alternatives through bank transfer  
* A public Wish Wall  
* A direct WhatsApp fulfillment path

The owner then shares one link through WhatsApp, Instagram, X, Facebook, group chats, communities, or direct messages.

Visitors do not need an account.

They can:

* View the Birthday Page  
* Browse the owner's photos  
* Read public birthday wishes  
* Select a photo  
* Write their own wish  
* Choose whether the wish is public or private  
* Unlock the wishlist after publishing the wish  
* View gift options  
* View purchase links  
* View bank transfer details  
* Contact the birthday person through WhatsApp to fulfill a wish

The central product loop is:

Owner creates page → Owner shares page → Visitor opens page → Visitor leaves birthday wish → Wishlist unlocks → Visitor explores gifts → Visitor contacts owner through WhatsApp → Visitor is invited to create their own Birthday Page.

The product does not initially attempt to become a payment marketplace or gift delivery service.

Its job is to create the birthday page, collect wishes, organize gift requests, and make coordination easy.

# **2\. Problem Statement**

Birthday celebrations in Nigeria frequently happen across multiple channels.

A birthday celebrant may:

* Post pictures on WhatsApp Status  
* Receive messages through WhatsApp  
* Receive Instagram comments and DMs  
* Share account details privately  
* Tell different friends what they want  
* Send Jumia or vendor links manually  
* Answer repeated questions about gift preferences  
* Receive wishes scattered across several platforms

There is no central destination containing the person's birthday content, wishes, gift preferences, purchase links, and direct contact path.

This creates several problems.

## **For the birthday person**

They repeatedly answer:

* "What do you want for your birthday?"  
* "What size do you wear?"  
* "Where can I buy it?"  
* "Can I just send you money?"  
* "Send your account number."  
* "Which exact one do you want?"

Their birthday wishes also disappear inside WhatsApp chats, Instagram DMs, status replies, and social comments.

## **For friends and visitors**

Gift giving involves unnecessary coordination.

Someone may want to buy a particular item but not know:

* Which version the person wants  
* Where to buy it  
* The expected price  
* Whether someone else already bought it  
* Whether cash would be preferred  
* How to contact the person quickly

## **Product opportunity**

The Birthday Page becomes a temporary digital home for the celebration.

Instead of sharing several messages, the owner shares one link.

That link answers:

1. When is the birthday?  
2. What does the person want?  
3. What are other people saying?  
4. How can I leave my own wish?  
5. How can I buy something?  
6. How can I send money?  
7. How can I speak directly with the celebrant?

The product should feel simple enough for someone to create a page within five minutes.

# **19\. Wishlist Gate Architecture**

The gate must not rely only on React state.

Bad implementation:

if hasSubmittedWish:  
    showWishlist()

Someone could bypass that client logic by directly calling the Supabase API.

Recommended architecture:

Visitor  
→ Submit wish through protected server function  
→ Server validates submission  
→ Server inserts wish  
→ Server generates page-specific access token  
→ Server stores only the token hash  
→ Token returned to visitor  
→ Browser stores token for that Birthday Page  
→ Wishlist request includes token  
→ Server validates token  
→ Server confirms page is still published  
→ Server returns wishlist data

The access token can expire after a reasonable period such as 24 hours or after the birthday campaign ends.

The token only grants access to that specific page's wishlist.

It does not authenticate the visitor as a platform user.

Supabase supports RLS for both authenticated and anonymous database access, and its Data API security model combines database grants with RLS. This makes it important to restrict the base tables rather than relying on frontend visibility rules.

# **47\. Row Level Security Strategy**

RLS should be enabled for every exposed application table.

Do not give anonymous visitors broad access to base tables.

## **profiles**

Authenticated owner:

SELECT own profile  
UPDATE own profile

Anonymous:

No access.

## **account\_entitlements**

Authenticated owner:

SELECT own entitlement

Anonymous:

No access.

Updates:

Server/admin only.

## **birthday\_pages**

Authenticated owner:

SELECT own pages  
INSERT own pages  
UPDATE own pages  
DELETE own pages

Anonymous:

Do not provide unrestricted base-table access.

Expose safe published-page information through a view, RPC, or server function.

This prevents sensitive fields such as WhatsApp numbers from being exposed prematurely.

## **page\_photos**

Owner:

Full management of photos belonging to their page.

Visitor:

Read photos for published pages.

## **bank\_accounts**

Owner:

CRUD own accounts.

Visitor:

No direct anonymous access.

Bank details should only be returned after wishlist access validation.

## **wishlist\_items**

Owner:

CRUD items belonging to their pages.

Visitor:

No direct anonymous SELECT.

Visitor access happens through a server-controlled function after the wish gate.

## **wishes**

Owner:

Read every wish belonging to their pages, including private wishes.

Owner:

Moderate their wishes.

Visitor:

Do not allow unrestricted direct SELECT.

Expose only public and visible wishes through a safe public endpoint or view.

Visitor wish creation should preferably occur through a server function rather than unrestricted table INSERT.

## **visitor\_page\_access**

Anonymous:

No direct read.

Server:

Create and validate access.

## **payments**

Authenticated user:

Read own payments.

Administrators:

Read relevant payments.

Anonymous:

No access.

## **manual\_payment\_submissions**

Authenticated user:

Read own submission.

Administrator:

Read and review all submissions.

Anonymous:

No access.

## **admin\_audit\_logs**

Administrators only.

# **49\. Spam and Abuse Prevention**

Unauthenticated wishes create an obvious spam target.

Multiple layers should be used.

## **49.1 Rate limiting**

Suggested starting rules:

Per IP and page:

Maximum 5 wish attempts within 10 minutes.

Per IP:

Maximum 20 successful wishes per day across the service.

Per visitor session and page:

Maximum 2 wishes during a short period.

These numbers should be adjusted using production data.

Avoid aggressive restrictions on shared office, campus, event, or home networks.

## **49.2 Bot challenge**

Do not show CAPTCHA to every visitor by default.

Trigger a bot challenge after suspicious behavior.

Examples:

* Repeated submissions  
* Extremely fast submissions  
* Multiple pages attacked from one source  
* Known automation patterns

## **49.3 Honeypot**

Add an invisible form field.

Human visitors leave it empty.

Bots filling every field can be rejected.

## **49.4 Minimum completion time**

Reject obviously automated form submissions completed unrealistically quickly.

Do not rely on this alone.

## **49.5 Content length**

Visitor name:

2 to 60 characters.

Message:

2 to 500 characters.

## **49.6 XSS protection**

Wish messages should render as plain text.

Never interpret visitor messages as HTML.

## **49.7 Link spam**

MVP recommendation:

Do not allow clickable links inside birthday wishes.

Birthday wishes do not require external URLs.

## **49.8 Visitor identity**

Visitor names are self-declared.

Never present a guest's name as a verified identity.

## **49.9 Owner moderation**

Owners must be able to:

* Hide  
* Delete  
* Restore

public wishes.

## **49.10 Platform moderation**

Add a report mechanism if public abuse becomes common.

This can start in Phase 2 unless required during beta.

`https://`

# **53\. Important Edge Cases**

## **Birthday already passed**

Allow page creation.

Display:

"Amani celebrated on August 15."

Owner can still collect wishes.

## **Birthday is today**

Display:

"Today is Amani's Birthday 🎉"

## **Visitor refreshes after submitting**

Wishlist access token should preserve access for its validity period.

## **Visitor opens another Birthday Page**

Access should not transfer.

Tokens must be page specific.

## **Page becomes unpublished**

Existing visitor tokens stop returning wishlist data.

## **Owner deletes selected photo**

Existing wish should still display gracefully.

Options:

* Preserve a thumbnail reference  
* Fall back to default page image

## **Owner changes WhatsApp number**

Future handoffs use the new number.

## **Invalid WhatsApp number**

Prevent publication until number validation succeeds.

## **Purchase URL unavailable**

Item can still use:

* Available anywhere  
* Bank transfer  
* WhatsApp coordination

## **Wishlist item fulfilled**

Show fulfilled state.

Disable new fulfillment CTA.

## **Multiple visitors want the same gift**

MVP does not reserve items automatically.

Owner coordinates through WhatsApp and marks the item fulfilled.

## **Owner upgrades after reaching Free limits**

Existing page remains unchanged.

Pro limits become immediately available.

## **Paystack succeeds but browser closes**

Webhook processing must still activate Pro.

Do not depend on the browser returning to the success page.

## **Webhook arrives twice**

Payment processing must be idempotent.

The same Paystack reference must never activate Pro twice or create duplicate payment records.

## **Manual payment approved twice**

Admin action must also be idempotent.

# **87\. Product Risks**

## **Risk 1: Visitors skip wishes and want gifts immediately**

Mitigation:

Backend-enforced wishlist gate.

## **Risk 2: Wishlist gate reduces gifting conversion**

Mitigation:

Keep wish form under 30 seconds.

Do not require account creation.

## **Risk 3: Spam wishes**

Mitigation:

Rate limits, honeypot, behavioral detection, moderation.

## **Risk 4: Users hesitate to share bank information**

Mitigation:

Make bank transfer optional.

## **Risk 5: Manual payment verification becomes operationally expensive**

Mitigation:

Keep Paystack as primary method and manual transfers as fallback.

## **Risk 6: Free plan provides too much value**

Mitigation:

Place meaningful customization, analytics, multiple pages, and branding removal behind Pro.

## **Risk 7: Pro does not feel valuable**

Mitigation:

Make premium page presentation and post-birthday memories major Pro benefits.

## **Risk 8: Visitors do not become owners**

Mitigation:

Place contextual creation CTA after they successfully submit a wish.

# **89\. Final Recommended Product Definition**

The MVP should be described simply as:

"Create one birthday link for your wishes, wishlist and gifts."

The product should avoid becoming a full e-commerce system during its first stage.

The core product should do six things very well:

1. Let anyone create a beautiful Birthday Page quickly.  
2. Make sharing easy through WhatsApp and social platforms.  
3. Let visitors leave messages without creating accounts.  
4. Require a birthday wish before revealing the wishlist.  
5. Make gifts easier through purchase links, bank transfer information and WhatsApp.  
6. Turn every birthday visitor into a possible future Birthday Page Owner.

If those six behaviors work smoothly, the platform already has a clear product loop, a paid upgrade path, and built-in distribution through every birthday page shared.
